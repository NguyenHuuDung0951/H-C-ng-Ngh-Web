- OUT STORY -

Learn More About Our History

For more than 40 years we have been delighting our guests with delicious dishes.

1978

Our founder, Peter Munijo, opened his first restaurant, Muelo, in downtown New York.

1997

The Muelo restaurant was renamed into El Gambino and focused on Italian and Spanish cuisine.

2005

We expanded our chain and opened two more El Gambino restaurants, in Boston and Chicago.

2020

TimeToGo named our chain "one of the best Italian restaurant chains in the United States".

About Our Restaurant

All the best dishes from the Mediterranean cuisine.

El Gambino is a chain of three top-rated Italian restaurants, conveniently located in New York, Boston, and Chicago. For four decades, El Gambino restaurant has been serving the local community with the world's famous cuisines. Our diversified menu has something special to meet any taste.

  • 🍃 Signature Mediterranean dishes;
  • 🍃 Many vegan and vegetarian options;
  • 🍃High quality of products in all our restaurants.

Our atmosphere is warm and inviting, perfect for any occasion, whether you're looking for a romantic dinner or a casual night out with friends. Come and experience the exquisite flavors of the Mediterranean with us!
